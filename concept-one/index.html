Know exactly what to expect before you arrive.

A home appointment can feel unfamiliar the first time. Clear practical information removes that uncertainty before someone books.

Location

South London home studio. The full address is included automatically with your booking confirmation, so your private home address isn't published publicly.

Parking

Free on-street parking is available close to the property. Your confirmation includes the easiest place to park and a simple arrival note.

Come to the main entrance and ring the studio bell. You'll be welcomed in and shown straight through to the dedicated nail room.
